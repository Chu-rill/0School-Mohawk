My Interest: Formula 1

Formula 1 is the pinnacle of motorsport, combining cutting edge engineering with elite driving talent to produce some of the most thrilling racing in the world. I have been following F1 for several years, drawn in by the technical side of the sport how teams develop their cars throughout a season, the strategy calls made during races, and the fine margins that separate winners from the rest of the grid.
